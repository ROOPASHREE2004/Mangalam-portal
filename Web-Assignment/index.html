Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of a laser cutter for sheet metal?
It is designed to cut various types of sheet metal with precision, allowing for intricate designs and shapes that are essential in manufacturing processes.
What are the benefits of using aluminum tubing in manufacturing?
Aluminum tubing is lightweight, strong, it is easy to work with cutting, bending, welding, has good thermal and electrical conductivity
How is aluminum tubing produced?
Aluminum tubing is typically made through extrusion. In this process, heated aluminum is forced through a die to create a tube shape
What are the common applications of aluminum tubing?
Aluminum tubing is widely used in construction (frames, railings), automotive and aerospace industries (structural components), HVAC systems, furniture, medical equipment
Can aluminum tubing be customized?
Yes, aluminum tubing can be customized in terms of size, thickness, shape, and finish. It can also be cut, bent, anodized, or coated to meet specific design
